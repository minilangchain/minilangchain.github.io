Sightread Privacy Policy

Operator: Grow & Grow & Grow Pty Ltd (ACN 635 108 299)

Effective date: 18 July 2026

App: Sightread (com.sim.chinesereader)

Bahasa Indonesia: Privacy policy (ID)

Summary

Sightread helps you practice reading Chinese. There are no user accounts and no advertising trackers. Study data stays on your device. When you generate stories, practice sentences, translations, or memory tips, that request is sent through our servers to AI providers so the feature can work.

Data we process

Stored on your device

  • Saved articles, flashcards, reading progress, and settings (local storage / IndexedDB)
  • An anonymous installation ID used only to enforce free daily generation limits

Clearing app data or reinstalling the app removes this local data and may reset your daily free limits.

Sent to our servers (and AI providers) when you use generation features

  • Story settings (genre, difficulty, length, vocabulary constraints)
  • Custom prompts, pasted article text, or URLs you choose to process
  • Sentences you ask to translate and words you ask for memory tips about
  • Your anonymous installation ID (and, if that ID is missing, your IP address) for daily quotas

We use AI providers such as DeepSeek and, when configured, Google Gemini. Content you submit for generation may be processed outside Indonesia (including Australia, Singapore, the United States, and other regions where those providers operate).

Operational logs (minimised)

  • Aggregate generation metadata (method, genre, success/failure, provider)
  • Daily usage counters keyed by installation ID (or IP fallback)
  • We do not retain the full text of your custom prompts in analytics logs

What we do not do

  • No account registration or login
  • No sale of personal data
  • No third-party advertising SDKs
  • No App Tracking Transparency / cross-app tracking

Retention

  • Device data: until you delete it or uninstall the app
  • Daily quota counters: kept for a short operational window (about 14 days) and then pruned
  • Aggregate generation metadata: kept for a short operational window (about 30 days) and then pruned
  • Content in transit to AI providers is processed to fulfil your request; we do not keep a permanent library of your generated stories on our servers

Your choices

  • Do not use generation features if you do not want content sent to AI providers
  • Clear app storage / reinstall to remove local study data and reset the installation ID
  • Contact us to ask about operational records associated with a report you send us

Indonesian personal data rights (UU PDP)

If you are in Indonesia, you may request information about processing related to a support enquiry you send us, ask for correction of inaccurate contact details you provide, or ask us to delete operational records tied to that enquiry where applicable. Contact: support@growgrowgrowapps.com.

Children

Sightread is intended for general audiences learning Chinese. It is not directed at children under 13. Parents/guardians who believe a child submitted content to us can contact support for help.
